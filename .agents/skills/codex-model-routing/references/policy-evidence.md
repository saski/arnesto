# Policy evidence and provenance

This local synthesis makes the skill independent of the original filesystem.
It derives from two user-supplied documents dated 2026-09-06:

- `guia-modelos-y-effort-2026-09.md`: operational model, effort, continuity,
  and Fast policy.
- `informe-gasto-julio-septiembre-2026-09.md`: consumption analysis for
  July 1 through September 6, 2026, before the report's own task.

The operational policy starts with Terra Medium, uses Luna for clear repeatable
work, Sol for complexity or risk, and reserves Astra for exceptionally difficult
end-to-end delivery. More effort must solve a reasoning need; continuity needs
a handoff and a fresh bounded unit. Fast buys speed at additional cost.

## What the historical report supports

The report analyzed 1,493 comparable turns across 236 rollouts and excluded
1,228 replayed subagent turns. It estimated 28,482.9 equivalent credits.
Sol accounted for 80.5% and Terra for 15.1%; Sol High and xhigh concentrated the
August spending peak. Astra had only 55 turns, all in September, totaling
378.1 equivalent credits. This supports monitoring expensive routes, not a
causal claim about model quality or a universal saving estimate.

Tasks, tools, effort, and session sizes differed. Accepted outcomes and human
correction time were absent. Fast could not be reliably inferred and was not
included in the estimates. Equivalent credits were not an invoice. Do not
rank quality from credits per turn or extrapolate Astra's small sample.

No historical price table is embedded here: rates and availability need live
verification, and the skill remains useful without those volatile numbers.
